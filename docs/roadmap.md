# JobPins — Product Roadmap

This roadmap consolidates **all future-oriented plans that were consistently discussed and aligned with the JobPins vision**, organized by priority and maturity. Speculative or abandoned ideas have been excluded.

---

## Completed

- Core product vision defined (location-first job discovery)
- Bangalore chosen as initial market
- V1 feature scope locked and implemented
- Frontend and backend fully functional end-to-end
- Employer authentication (signup/login)
- Job posting with Google Maps–based location extraction
- Public job discovery via map + list
- Employer “My Jobs” dashboard with delete support
- Backend stabilized and frozen
- Frontend foundation fixes completed (auth hydration, routing stability, loaders)
- Moved to Supabase Auth + row-level security; Render backend retired (October 2026)
- Financial baseline and monthly burn clarified

---

## Short-Term (Next)

High-priority actions required to move from “working product” to “launched product”.

### Product & UX

- Final UI/UX polish (visual consistency, typography, spacing, copy clarity)
- Freeze frontend after final polish
- Prepare product for first-time user clarity (no feature additions)

### Infrastructure

- Export finalized frontend code from Lovable
- Deploy frontend on Vercel
- Connect custom domain to Vercel
- Remove Lovable branding from live product
- Cancel Lovable hosting after successful migration

### Launch

- Soft launch to limited audience (e.g., LinkedIn network)
- Collect qualitative feedback from early users
- Observe employer usage patterns and job posting behavior

---

## Mid-Term

Planned once initial usage and validation data is available.

### Monetization Preparation

- Define pricing strategy (likely pay-per-job and/or featured listings)
- Validate willingness to pay through conversations and manual outreach
- Track key signals:
    - Repeat job postings
    - Employers asking about pricing
    - Demand for visibility or priority placement

### Product Improvements (Incremental)

- Improved empty states and guidance based on user feedback
- Minor UX enhancements that do not affect core logic
- Basic operational tooling for managing early users (manual processes)

---

## Long-Term

Deferred until clear traction and validation are achieved.

### Product Expansion

- Introduce monetization flows (automated payments)
- Featured job listings
- Employer insights or basic analytics
- Multi-city expansion beyond Bangalore

### Platform Maturity

- Operational automation (notifications, emails, SMS)
- More advanced employer tooling
- Scalable infrastructure upgrades as traffic grows

---

## Open Questions

- What level of UI polish is “good enough” before export and launch?
- What specific success metrics will determine V1 validation?
- Which employer segment shows strongest early traction (startups, SMBs, recruiters)?
- When should monetization be introduced without harming adoption?
- At what point should the product expand beyond Bangalore?
